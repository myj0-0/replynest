# Quick start

1. Sign into the [console](https://console.replynest.cn/login), confirm the business and connect [WeChat](wechat-official.md) or [Web Chat](website-chat.md).
2. Send a test message, reply manually in the [workspace](agent-workspace.md) and verify receipt.
3. Prepare [knowledge](knowledge-copilot.md), adapt [templates](template-market.md), invite members and set notifications.
4. Choose an [AI mode](automatic-reception.md) and separately verify consent and readiness. Check points and purchases in [Billing](billing.md).

After signing in to the operations console, confirm the current workspace, then configure channels, knowledge, members, and alerts.

- **Operations console** manages integrations, knowledge, policies, members, billing, and audit records.
- **Agent workspace** handles conversations, AI suggestions, edits, and final replies.

Use an individual account for every team member so actions remain attributable.
