# Templates: claim, import and share

Select a type in the [marketplace](https://console.replynest.cn/template-market).

October 8, 2026 capture. Quick replies become editable phrases; knowledge imports are reviewable drafts; Studio imports are new drafts requiring simulation, publication and separate scheduling consent if applicable.

Check author, version, price and license, claim/buy, then import. Replace sample brands, contacts, prices and policies with verified business content. Paid templates use cash only, not gifts or AI points. After timeouts check state before retrying.

Imports do not automatically publish knowledge, run code, install schedules, read customers or send. Official starters are published by the ReplyNest Official business. Free does not imply suitability or permission to copy third-party content.

Submit your own template with version, description and license after removing private data, secrets and unauthorized content. Price CNY 0–99, zero free; platform review precedes publication.

Seller revenue is separately recorded, currently frozen seven days with an adjustable 2% sales fee. Approved refunds return to buyer cash and reverse earnings/fees. Already-paid-out deficits become seller debt offset against later earnings, blocking new payouts until cleared. Payouts require approval and currently a separate 1% fee. Consult transaction terms and [Billing](billing.md).
